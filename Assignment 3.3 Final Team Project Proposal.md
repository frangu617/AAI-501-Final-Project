# Introduction

For our team project, we plan to develop an AI-based system that predicts whether an online shopping website visitor will complete a purchase based on their browsing behavior.

Today, many people visit online shopping websites, but not everyone completes a purchase. Understanding customer browsing behavior can help online businesses improve their websites and provide a better shopping experience.

We plan to use the Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention Dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository. The dataset contains 12,330 shopping sessions with information such as the number of pages visited, time spent on different pages, visitor type, traffic source, and other website activity.

# Related Course Topics

We will explore the appropriate algorithms and techniques during the project development based on the dataset and experimental results. As of now, the following are good candidates:

- Classification- We will use classification to predict whether a visitor will complete a purchase (True) or not (False)

- Preparing and organizing the dataset for Machine Learning

- Analyzing customer behavior and identifying patterns using data and charts

- Training machine learning models and comparing their performance to understand how accurately they predict purchasing behavior

# Expected system behavior and Problems to be Handled

We expect our system to analyze online shopping sessions and predict whether a visitor is likely to complete a purchase. Some examples of expected system behavior include

- Predict whether a visitor will make a purchase based on their browsing activity.

- Identify whether the number of product pages visited and time spent browsing are related to purchase decisions.

- Compare the behavior of new and returning visitors.

- Understand how bounce rates and exit rates are related to purchasing behavior.

# Issue we expect to focus on

During this project, we expect to focus on the following issues

- Understanding and preparing the dataset for Machine learning and handling numerical and categorical data.

- Understanding why some visitors make purchases while others do not and addressing the imbalance between purchase and non-purchase sessions

- Comparing the performance (We are not at this stage to use Logistic regression/Random Forest; we will try to figure out in coming weeks)

- Identifying which dataset features are most useful for predicting purchasing intentions
